# Why Aguna Component

**Source File:** [components/WhyAguna.tsx](file:///c:/Users/pranav/aguna-website/Aguna-portfolio/components/WhyAguna.tsx)  
**Primary Purpose:** Presents a high-level summary of the 5 core value propositions that make Aguna a reliable security partner on the Services page.

---

## 1. Visual Specification & Styling

*   **Background:** Sits on a light blue-200 canvas (`bg-blue-200 py-12`) that provides a soft, warm contrast between dark page sections.
*   **Card Container:** Renders a central, wide white card (`bg-white/90 backdrop-blur-md border border-blue-300/30 rounded-3xl p-6 md:p-10 shadow-xl`) that lifts slightly on hover.
*   **List Layout:** Organizes the 5 reasons into a vertical stack with generous spacing, using blue checkmark icons to draw the eye.

---

## 2. The 5 Value Propositions

1.  **Risk-based, outcome-driven security approach:** Focuses on business risk mitigation and concrete safety metrics rather than checklists.
2.  **Manual and automated testing expertise:** Combines industry-standard scanners with manual, expert-led exploitation to uncover complex vulnerabilities.
3.  **Clear, actionable reporting and remediation guidance:** Provides simple, direct explanations of risks and step-by-step guidelines for developers to fix vulnerabilities.
4.  **Proactive security mindset, not reactive support:** Emphasizes continuous monitoring and prevention over emergency response.
5.  **Solutions tailored to your business and environment:** Rejects generic templates to design security controls around the client's specific architecture and workflows.
