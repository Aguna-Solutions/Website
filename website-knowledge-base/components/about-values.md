# About Values Component

**Source File:** [components/AboutValues.tsx](file:///c:/Users/pranav/aguna-website/Aguna-portfolio/components/AboutValues.tsx)  
**Primary Purpose:** Documents the 6 core "Operating Principles" that guide Aguna's consulting, engineering, and client relationships on the About page.

---

## 1. Visual Specification & Styling

*   **Background:** Sits on a dark canvas (`bg-black`) with a subtle top border (`border-t border-white/10`) to separate it from preceding sections.
*   **Layout:** Renders a 2-column grid (`grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-x-16 gap-y-12`) that centers the content into a readable, double-column list.
*   **Card Container:** The principles are styled as minimal lists with a prominent blue-highlighted icon badge (`bg-blue-500/10 text-blue-400 border-blue-500/20`) on the left that brightens on hover (`group-hover:bg-blue-500/20`).

---

## 2. The 6 Operating Principles

### 1. Integrity of Execution
*   **Description:** "We prioritize absolute transparency and technical accountability in every security deployment, ensuring results are measurable and verifiable."
*   **Icon:** ShieldCheck (Lucide ShieldCheck)

### 2. Operational Resilience
*   **Description:** "Our strategy is built on long-term risk management and the preservation of business continuity under even the most dynamic threat conditions."
*   **Icon:** Activity (Lucide Activity)

### 3. Decisive Clarity
*   **Description:** "We distill complex multi-vector threats into actionable intelligence, enabling leadership to make confident, data-driven security decisions."
*   **Icon:** Eye (Lucide Eye)

### 4. Security by Design
*   **Description:** "We integrate advanced security protocols into the fundamental architecture of every project, moving beyond reactive fixes to systemic protection."
*   **Icon:** Layers (Lucide Layers)

### 5. Evidence-Based Rigor
*   **Description:** "Our analysis is rooted in empirical data and proven methodologies, ensuring our defensive postures are robust against real-world exploitation."
*   **Icon:** Search (Lucide Search)

### 6. Direct Accountability
*   **Description:** "We operate as an extension of our clients' teams, taking full ownership of security outcomes and the success of the risk-mitigation lifecycle."
*   **Icon:** CheckCircle (Lucide CheckCircle)
