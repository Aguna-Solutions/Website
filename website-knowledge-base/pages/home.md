## Page Summary

The Home page welcomes users with a premium dark cyber-theme, displaying a large typographical headline with rotating typewriter values. It structures the user journey through risk awareness (a glowing warning banner), solution capability (a 2x2 grid explaining how Aguna helps), brand validation (an infinite marquee of client logos and differentiators), and institutional verification (a grid displaying verified ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type I & II audits).
