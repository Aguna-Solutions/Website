## Page Summary

The About page serves as a comprehensive corporate overview. It begins with a dual-column layout detailing "Who We Are" and "Digital Transformation" beside a looping high-tech video showcase. It then transitions into "How We Work" (a 4-step client lifecycle timeline on a clean white background), outlines 6 key "Operating Principles" in a high-contrast dark grid, and finishes with a directory of 8 key technology partners and institutional affiliations.
